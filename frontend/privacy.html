Privacy Policy

Drive Uploader is a private, single-owner tool used to upload files to the owner's own Google Drive.

The app uses the Google Drive scope drive.file only to create a folder and upload files chosen by the owner. It does not read, list or access any other files in Google Drive.

The app does not collect, sell or share personal data with third parties. Files are sent directly from the owner's browser to Google Drive. Login credentials and API tokens are stored only on the owner's server.

Contact: use the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
